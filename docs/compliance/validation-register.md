# Compliance validation register

The following items require qualified Indian legal, pharmaceutical, tax, privacy, or payment advice.
They are deliberately not encoded as assumed requirements:

- Licences and evidence required from pharmacies, dealers, and transport partners
- Pharmaceutical product master-data and batch traceability obligations
- GST invoice, credit-note, place-of-supply, rounding, HSN, CGST, SGST, and IGST rules
- KYC evidence, beneficial-owner, representative, expiry, and re-verification requirements
- Marketplace checkout, payment collection, refunds, dealer settlement, and nodal/escrow treatment
- Data retention, deletion, breach handling, consent, and residency obligations
- SMS registration, sender identity, templates, and transactional-message classifications
- Audit-log retention and access requirements

Each validated requirement must link to an owner, authoritative advice or source, effective date,
and an architecture or product decision before it becomes executable policy.

PH1.2 therefore exposes drug licence, GST registration, PAN, business registration, bank document,
and other as neutral evidence categories. It does not claim that any category is legally required
or sufficient, and it applies no automatic expiry decision. The temporary product rule requiring at
least one supporting file before submission must be replaced by a jurisdiction-aware policy after
qualified review.
